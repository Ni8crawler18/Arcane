FIELD: The problem you are solving
(max 150 words / 1200 characters)
================================================================

Cancer survivors need years of structured follow-up - recurrence surveillance, side-effect monitoring - because early recurrence detection is one of the strongest predictors of survival. Today this tracking is manual: oncologists managing hundreds of patients rely on memory or spreadsheets to know who is due for a scan. Follow-ups slip through silently - there's no alert when a patient doesn't come back. When they do return, doctors reconstruct a treatment history from scattered notes before deciding anything. This affects patients whose survival depends on timely surveillance, and oncologists already stretched by India's severe doctor-to-patient ratio. The gap isn't clinical knowledge - it's that follow-up has no system behind it.

FIELD: Your solution, and its name
(max 150 words / 1200 characters)
================================================================

OncoFollow analyses each patient's treatment record to flag who is due or overdue for follow-up, based on cancer type and stage, and ranks them by clinical urgency - not whoever calls first. When a follow-up is due, it automatically sends a personalised reminder referencing the patient's own case, generated from the short note their doctor left after the last visit - the same note lets a doctor recall a patient's status in seconds before a call. Access is enforced as policy, not convention: sensitive oncology records stay restricted to the treating doctor by design.

FIELD: How you will build it
(max 80 words / 600 characters)
================================================================

An AI agent (Strands Agents SDK) reads each patient's notes and drafts the follow-up message. Cedar policy-based authorization governs every action it takes - only "read notes" and "send a reminder," nothing else. Records live in a NoSQL store (DynamoDB-style) with OpenSearch for fast note retrieval. A Step Functions-style scheduler tracks due dates and triggers SMS/WhatsApp outreach. Backend in Python.

FIELD: Existing product/technology vs. new hackathon build
(max 150 words / 1200 characters)
================================================================

We already have a working prototype from an earlier hackathon: patient records, doctor notes, and Cedar-guarded automated reminders via an AI agent (Strands Agents SDK). It treats every follow-up the same, with no sense of clinical urgency. For this hackathon we're adding oncology-specific logic: follow-up windows per cancer type and treatment stage, a risk-based prioritisation layer ranking overdue patients by clinical consequence (not just days overdue), and outreach that adapts to treatment stage - a newly diagnosed patient and a five-year survivor should never get the same reminder.
